# YG-Tech site refresh

Homepage changes:
- Starts with a broad introduction to YG-Tech instead of immediately promoting Invoice Studio.
- Adds quick cards for software, downloads, quizzes, learning resources, datasets, and future tools.
- Keeps Invoice Studio as featured software but moves detailed features/screenshots/install notes into a collapsible section.
- Adds left/right controls to both screenshot rails while retaining swipe/trackpad scrolling and full-size lightbox viewing.

New public pages:
- `privacy.html`
- `terms.html`
- `support.html`
- `faq.html`
- `changelog.html`

The new pages are linked from the homepage and shared navigation/footer areas.
