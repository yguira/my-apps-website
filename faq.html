Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Invoice Studio and YG-Tech resources.

Invoice Studio

Does Invoice Studio require an account?

No. The current Android and Windows editions are designed to work without creating a YG-Tech account.

Can I use my own company logo?

Yes. The Android app includes business-profile settings where you can choose and save a company logo for invoices.

Does the Android app support French?

Yes. The Android edition supports English and French and includes localized onboarding and invoice labels.

Can I export invoices?

Yes. The Android version supports PDF and CSV export, printing, and Android sharing. The Windows version also generates professional invoice documents.

Why does Android warn me when installing the APK from this website?

Direct APK installation is outside Google Play, so Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. A Google Play release is being prepared.

Why must the Windows _internal folder stay beside the EXE?

The desktop package includes runtime files that the executable needs. Moving only the EXE can prevent the application from starting.

Website resources

Are the practice datasets free to use?

The CSV practice datasets distributed directly by YG-Tech on this site are synthetic practice files created for learning and experimentation.

Will more tools be added?

Yes. YG-Tech is intended to grow beyond Invoice Studio with additional software, learning material, productivity tools, and practical resources as they are ready.
