Invoice Studio by YG-Tech

Professional invoicing made simple.

📱 Android 8+ 🖥️ Windows 10 / 11 🌍 English & French 🔒 Offline-first

Create professional invoices, save customer information, scan receipts, track previous invoices, manage payment status, and export documents directly from your device.

Invoice Studio is designed for small businesses, independent contractors, technicians, service providers, freelancers, and entrepreneurs who want professional invoicing without complicated accounting software.

📄

Professional Invoices

Create detailed invoices with your business information, customer details, services, products, taxes, discounts, and payment status.

👥

Customer Management

Save customer details and quickly reuse them when creating future invoices.

📷

Receipt Scanner

Scan or import receipts and review detected products, receipt numbers, quantities, and prices before adding them to an invoice.

📤

PDF & CSV Export

Save professional PDF invoices or export invoice information in spreadsheet-friendly CSV format.

🌍

English & French

Use the application and generate invoices in English or French.

🏢

Your Business Branding

Add your company name, contact information, business settings, and company logo.

💰

Payment Tracking

Keep track of unpaid, partially paid, paid, overdue, draft, and void invoices.

📱

Offline Friendly

Core invoice creation and management work locally without requiring an account.
